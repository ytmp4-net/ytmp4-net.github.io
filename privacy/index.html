Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use YTMP4 at ytmp4-net.github.io and the converter service behind it. The short version: we don't ask for personal details, we don't sell data, and we keep only what's needed to run the service and stop abuse.

Information you give us

The only thing you give the converter is the YouTube link you paste. It's used to look up the video and prepare the file you ask for. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. When the page talks to our conversion server, the server records standard technical data: IP address, browser type, the time of the request, the requested link and the response status. We use this to keep the service running, fix errors, count usage and block abuse such as automated scraping. Logs are kept for about two weeks and then deleted.
  • Hosting. The page itself is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Content delivery and security. Our conversion server is behind Cloudflare, which processes requests to protect against attacks and may set strictly necessary security cookies.

Cookies and advertising

YTMP4 doesn't set its own tracking cookies. The page shows ads from third-party advertising networks (such as Adsterra). These partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technology to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad and measure performance. Their use of that data is governed by their own privacy policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the converter keeps working without them.

We remember one small preference in your browser's session storage: whether you closed the bottom ad bar, so it doesn't come back during the same visit. It never leaves your device.

Videos and files

We don't build a library of the videos people convert. Files are prepared on request, delivered to your browser, and removed from our server automatically after a short time. We don't link conversions to any personal profile.

How we use information

  • to provide the conversion and download you request,
  • to keep the service secure and prevent abuse,
  • to understand overall usage (for example, how many conversions happen per day),
  • to answer your messages.

We don't sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Children

YTMP4 isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its use. Because we don't keep accounts, the only data we might hold is in short-lived server logs or in emails you sent us. Write to admin@ytmp4.store and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Privacy questions: admin@ytmp4.store.
