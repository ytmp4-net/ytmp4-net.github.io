About YTMP4

Last updated: November 11, 2026

YTMP4 is a small, independent web tool for saving YouTube videos as MP4 video or MP3 audio. It's made by the same team that runs YTMP4 at ytmp4.store, where the converter is available in 13 languages. This github.io page is the lightweight English edition.

Why we built it

We were tired of downloader sites that hide the real button between three fake ones, force an app install, or redirect you to a different page every time you tap. YTMP4 does one thing: you paste a link, you see every quality the video actually has, and you download the one you want. Nothing else gets in the way.

How it works

When you press Convert, the page sends the link to our server, which asks YouTube which formats exist for that video. Our server then prepares the file you choose and sends it straight to your browser. We don't keep a library of videos, and prepared files are removed after a short time.

What we believe in

  • No tricks. No pop-ups, no fake buttons, no forced installs.
  • No accounts. You don't need to give us your name or email to use the tool.
  • Respect for creators. We ask everyone to download only what they have the right to keep. See our Terms of Use.

Get in touch

Questions, bug reports or ideas are welcome at admin@ytmp4.store. You can also find the project on GitHub.
