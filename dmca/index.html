DMCA & Copyright

Last updated: November 11, 2026

YTMP4 respects the rights of creators and copyright owners. We don't host or store videos: files are fetched from YouTube when a user requests them and removed automatically afterwards. Still, if you believe our service is being used to infringe your copyright, we'll act quickly.

Sending a notice

Email admin@ytmp4.store with the subject "DMCA Notice" and include:

  • your name, organization (if any) and contact details,
  • a description of the copyrighted work,
  • the YouTube URL(s) of the content you want blocked from conversion on YTMP4,
  • a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law,
  • a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act for them,
  • your physical or electronic signature.

What we do

After receiving a complete notice, we block the listed videos from being converted on YTMP4, usually within two to three working days, and reply to confirm. Because the original video lives on YouTube, you may also want to contact YouTube directly through its own copyright process to have it removed there.

Repeat abuse

We may block users who repeatedly use the service to infringe copyright.
